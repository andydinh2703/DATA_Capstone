## About Success for Geneva's Children

Success for Geneva's Children is a community coalition based in Geneva, New York, dedicated to improving outcomes for children and families in the region.

### Mission

> The mission of Success for Geneva's Children is to mobilize the community to improve the health and well-being of all our children and their families.

### Statement of Purpose

> "Through understanding the needs and interests of children and their parents, we collectively bring resources to improving their quality of life. We strive to build effective interventions and supports, knowing their profound and beneficial impact on the individual child, the family, and the community."

### About This Dashboard

This interactive dashboard was built in partnership with Success for Geneva's Children to support data-driven community conversations. It brings together public data on child health, family stability, and academic readiness to help Geneva stakeholders understand how local children are faring — and where the community can focus its efforts.
