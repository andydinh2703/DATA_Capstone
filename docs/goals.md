## Project Goals

The overarching goal of this project is to allow the broader Geneva community to explore various relationships and determine whether Geneva's children are adequately prepared to be successful later on in life.

This dashboard addresses three core questions:

1. **Determine if Geneva's children are born healthy.**
   Examine infant mortality rates and low birth weight data across Geneva, Ontario County, and New York State.

2. **Determine if Geneva's children have a stable home.**
   Explore family poverty rates, household structure, health insurance coverage, income levels, and access to public assistance programs.

3. **Determine if Geneva's children have a ready mind.**
   Compare ELA and Math proficiency scores and English language learner rates between Geneva and other school districts across the state.
