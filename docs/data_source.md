## Data Sources

All data used in this dashboard comes from publicly available sources. Below is a summary of each source and the datasets it provides.

---

### NYS Department of Health

- **Low birth weight** — Percentage of low birth weight births for each county in New York State
- **Infant mortality** — Infant mortality rates for Geneva, Ontario County, and New York State

---

### NYS Education Department

- **Academic proficiency** — ELA and Math proficiency rates for the Geneva City School District, broken down by grade level (grades 3, 4, and 8)
- **English language learners** — Number and percentage of ELL students enrolled in the Geneva City School District over time

---

### U.S. Census Bureau (American Community Survey)

- **Population & income** — Population totals and median household income for Geneva, Ontario County, and New York State
- **Family poverty** — Percentage of families living below the federal poverty line in Geneva, Ontario County, and New York State
- **Family structure** — Breakdown of household types (two-parent, single mother, single father) for children under age 6
- **Health insurance coverage** — Percentage of residents with health insurance coverage in Geneva, Ontario County, and New York State
- **SNAP & TANF benefits** — Number of households receiving SNAP food assistance and TANF cash assistance in Geneva and Ontario County

---

### Syracuse.com

- **Statewide school district proficiency** — ELA and Math proficiency rates for all school districts in New York State, used for district-wide comparisons against Geneva
