## Dashboard Guide

This dashboard is organized around three core questions about Geneva's children. Each tab focuses on one of these questions, with sub-tabs that let you dig into the data behind it.

---

### Healthy Born

*Are Geneva's children born healthy?*

This tab looks at birth outcomes, comparing Geneva to Ontario County and New York State.

- **Infant Mortality** — Tracks the rate of infant deaths per 1,000 live births over time. Use the year slider and location checkboxes to focus your view.
- **Low Birth Weight** — Shows the percentage of babies born under 2,500 grams. Includes a county-level heat map of New York State and a trend chart comparing Ontario County to randomly sampled counties.

---

### Stable Home

*Do Geneva's children have a stable home?*

This tab examines family economic stability and support systems.

- **Poverty** — Family poverty rates over time for Geneva, Ontario County, and NYS. Includes a slope chart showing change over the selected period.
- **Health Insurance** — Percentage of residents with health insurance coverage, with the same trend and comparison views.
- **Family Structure** — Breakdown of households with children under 6 by type (two-parent, single mother, single father). Color-coded stat cards show whether Geneva's trends are moving in a favorable or unfavorable direction.
- **Population & Income** — Indexed population and median income trends (base year 2014 = 100) to show relative growth or decline.
- **SNAP & TANF** — Number and percentage of households receiving food assistance (SNAP) and cash assistance (TANF) in Geneva and Ontario County.

---

### Ready Mind

*Are Geneva's children ready to learn?*

This tab focuses on academic readiness and educational outcomes.

- **School Proficiency** — ELA and Math proficiency rates by school district across New York State. Includes a county heat map, a bar chart ranking districts within the selected county, and a grade-level trend line for Geneva City SD.
- **English Language Learners** — Tracks the number and proportion of ELL students in the Geneva City School District over time.
